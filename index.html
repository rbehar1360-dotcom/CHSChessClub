The Club

Our goal here at Chess Club is to meet with like-minded people, have fun, and play chess. We aim to teach others how to learn and play one of the oldest games in history while enjoying themselves.

Why Chess Club

Three reasons to come play.

01

Learn

Even if you don't know how to play, we wil teach you!

02

Meet people

Our club is new and growing, if you want to meet people, Chess club is the place to go!

03

Competition

We have monthly brackets and play against each other daily!
